## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/oh-no-thello/

**Oh No, Thello!** is Reversi with Minesweeper hidden under the board.

You play Black against the computer on the usual 8 by 8 board. Place a disc so it traps a line of White's discs, and they flip to your colour. Most discs at the end wins.

But there are eight mines under the board, never in the middle 4 by 4. Every disc shows how many mines touch its square. Place a disc on a mine and your move still happens, flips and all, and then that square and the eight around it explode: every disc there is gone, and the mine with them. The computer cannot see the mines either.

**Daily puzzle:** everyone gets the same mines on the same day. Your score is your disc count at the end, and the game keeps your best for each day.

**Controls:** click or tap a square. Arrows or WASD move a cursor and Enter or Space places. A gamepad works too (d-pad or stick, A or Start).

Free, in the browser.
